# Fictional job posting — Customer Success Coordinator

This posting is invented for demonstrating the workflow.

**Employer:** Cedar Software  
**Location:** Remote within Spain  
**Role:** Customer Success Coordinator

## The role

Help new customers get started, respond clearly to questions, coordinate follow-ups with the product team, and improve the team's onboarding materials.

## Requirements

- Excellent written and spoken customer communication.
- Experience organizing several requests or tasks at once.
- Ability to document repeatable processes and explain them to others.
- At least one year of direct B2B software customer support or customer success experience.

## Preferred

- Experience using a CRM.
- Experience measuring customer onboarding or support outcomes.
